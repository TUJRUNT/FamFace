# Privacy Policy (Template)

1. Introduction
We respect your privacy. This policy explains how `FamFace` collects, uses, and discloses information.

2. Information We Collect
- Account information: username, profile picture (stored locally by default).
- Messages and files: messages and uploaded files are relayed and stored on the server to support group chat.
- Usage data: connection logs, timestamps.

3. How We Use Information
- To provide chat and call functionality.
- To process file uploads and deliver messages.

4. Third-Party Services
- This app may use third-party libraries such as Socket.io and Simple-Peer. Their privacy practices apply.

5. Data Retention
- Messages and uploads are retained on the server until manually cleared or a storage retention policy is applied.

- You can delete your account data by contacting the administrator or using provided account controls.
